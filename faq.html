Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before you begin.

A few things people often want to know about career coaching and career documents.

What career services do you offer?

Services include one-to-one career coaching and professional documents such as resumes, CVs, cover letters, LinkedIn profiles, and professional bios.

Can I request just one document?

Yes. You can discuss an individual document or a combination of services, depending on what would be most useful.

How does career coaching work?

Coaching is tailored to your goals and can cover career direction, transitions, interview preparation, or offer conversations.

Do you use templates?

Your materials are developed around your experience, goals, and voice rather than filled into a generic template.

How are fees decided?

Fees depend on the scope of the work. You will receive a clear written scope and fee before any engagement begins.

How do I get started?

Send a short note about what you need. We will arrange an initial conversation to discuss fit and possible next steps.
