Why leadership hires go wrong

The problem with most executive searches

Most failed executive placements don't fail because the wrong resume got selected — they fail because the role was not properly defined, the search moved too fast to properly reference-check, or nobody agreed on what success actually looked like until six months in. Caldwell's process is built to close those gaps before a single candidate conversation happens.

Vague scorecards

Hiring panels often disagree on what the role actually requires. We surface and resolve that disagreement before outreach begins.

Speed over rigor

Urgent hires often skip proper reference calibration. We build urgency into the process without cutting the steps that predict long-term success.

Candidate overselling

Executives naturally present their best case. Structured interviews and references cut through the pitch to the pattern.

Cultural mismatch

Technical fit is necessary but not sufficient. We assess working style and operating rhythm as seriously as credentials.
