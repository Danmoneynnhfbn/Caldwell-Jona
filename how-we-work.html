How it works

A clear, collaborative process.

Start with your goals. Together, we will identify the support that makes sense and agree on a practical way forward.

Your engagement

From first conversation to next step.

1. Initial Conversation

Talk through your situation, goals, timeline, and the kind of support you are looking for.

2. Scope & Plan

Choose the service or combination of services that best fits your needs, and agree on scope, timing, and fees.

3. Discovery

Share the details of your experience, achievements, and priorities so the work reflects your story.

4. Coaching or Drafting

Work one-to-one on your career questions, or review the first draft of your resume, CV, or other document.

5. Review & Refine

Share feedback and refine the plan or documents together to make sure they are accurate and feel authentic.

6. Put It to Work

Leave with clearer next steps, polished materials, or both—and know where to get in touch if you need further help.

A personal process

You will know what happens next.

Every engagement starts with a direct conversation and a clear agreement about the work. No generic packages or unexplained process.
