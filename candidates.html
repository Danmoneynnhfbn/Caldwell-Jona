Why qualified executives get overlooked

The problem with most executive job searches

It's rarely a lack of qualifications that stalls an executive job search. More often, it's a resume that lists responsibilities instead of outcomes, a LinkedIn profile that undersells actual seniority, a network that has gone quiet, or an interview approach that worked at a lower level but does not land with a search committee.

Resumes written as job descriptions

Executive resumes need to show impact and scale, not task lists.

Underpowered LinkedIn presence

Many senior executives have profiles that do not reflect their true role or seniority.

A cold network

Years in one company often mean a dormant professional network. We help reactivate it strategically.

Interview approaches that don't scale

What worked at Director level often falls flat at VP or C-suite level.
